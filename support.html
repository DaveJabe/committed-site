Common questions

How do I restore my purchase?

Open Committed, bring up the Lifetime Unlock screen, and tap Restore purchase while signed in with the Apple ID you used to buy it.

How do backups work?

Backup syncs your data to your own private iCloud account. Manage it in the app’s Settings. Your data stays in your iCloud — the developer can’t access it.

How do hidden commitments stay private?

They’re protected by Face ID on your device. Your biometric data never leaves your iPhone.

Is my data private?

Yes. Committed collects no personal data and uses no analytics or tracking. See our Privacy Policy.
