Committed

Privacy Policy

Effective November 23, 2026

Committed (“the app,” “we,” “us”) is a personal sobriety and habit tracker. This policy explains, in plain language, what the app does with your information. The short version: your data stays on your device and, if you choose, in your own private iCloud. We never collect it, never see it, and never share or sell it. The one exception is feedback you choose to send us (see “Feedback you send us”).

Our approach: privacy by design

Committed is built to be local-first. The commitments you track, your start dates, milestones, notes, and settings are stored on your device. The only server we run receives nothing but the feedback you choose to send, and the app contains no third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, or telemetry of any kind.

We have no account system. You don’t create a login, and we never ask for your name, email address, or phone number to use the app.

Information the app stores on your device

To do its job, the app keeps the information you enter, including:

  • The commitments you create and their start dates
  • Milestones, including any custom milestones and goals you set
  • Notes or details you add to a commitment
  • Which commitments you choose to mark as hidden/private
  • Your appearance, widget, and app preferences
  • The names and phone numbers of up to three people you trust, if you add any (see “Resources and Meetings” below)

This information is stored locally on your device. It is never transmitted to us.

iCloud Sync (optional)

If you turn on iCloud Sync, the app keeps a copy of your commitments, milestones and progress, and a few of the app’s settings, in your own private iCloud account using Apple’s iCloud (CloudKit) service, so your devices signed in to the same Apple Account stay in step. This copy is held in your private iCloud database, which is accessible only to you through your Apple Account. We cannot access, read, or retrieve this data, and neither can any other user. Apple’s handling of your iCloud data is governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

iCloud Sync is off until you turn it on, during setup or in the app’s settings. You can turn it off at any time, and Delete from iCloud removes the synced copy.

Versions of Committed before iCloud Sync could keep one backup copy in your private iCloud. If you have one, the app can restore it, and Delete Old Backup removes it.

Hidden commitments and Face ID

The app lets you keep certain commitments private behind Face ID, Touch ID, or a passcode. Biometric authentication is performed entirely by your device’s operating system. Your biometric data never leaves your device and is never available to the app or to us — iOS only tells the app whether authentication succeeded.

In-app purchases

Committed offers a one-time Lifetime Unlock purchase. All purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment details, credit card number, or billing address. Apple’s handling of purchase information is governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Feedback you send us

Settings has a Send Feedback form. Nothing is sent unless you fill it in and tap Send. When you do, we receive what you wrote, the type of feedback you picked, and the email address you added, if you added one (it is optional, for a reply). So that we can understand the report, it also carries the app’s version and build, your device’s model and system version, its language and region setting, and whether the Lifetime Unlock is owned. Your commitments, notes and other data are not part of it.

The form sends this to a small server we run on Cloudflare, which files it as a private issue in our developer account on GitHub, where we read it. We use it only to answer you and to improve the app, and we delete it if you ask us to at the address below.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not collect your personal data, apart from feedback you choose to send us.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data with anyone.
  • We do not receive your information on any server we control, apart from that feedback.
  • We do not track you across apps or websites.

How your data is shared

Your data is not shared with us or any third party. The only places your information can travel are:

  • Your own device (local storage),
  • Your own private iCloud account (only if you turn on iCloud Sync), via Apple, and
  • Us, only for feedback you choose to send (see “Feedback you send us”).

Resources and Meetings

The Meetings screen lists links to the meeting finders of Alcoholics Anonymous, Narcotics Anonymous, SMART Recovery, Al-Anon and other recovery groups. Meetings opens each group’s own website or app. Committed doesn’t see what you do there, and that site’s own privacy policy applies.

The Resources screen holds Meetings, free helplines and a few free pages to read. A helpline is called or texted by your iPhone’s own Phone and Messages apps, and a page opens on that service’s own website. Committed doesn’t see what you say or read there.

The app does not ask for or use your location for this. The lists start from your device’s region setting, and a country you pick there is kept on your device. We keep no record of which links you open or which numbers you call.

People you trust. On iPhone you can add up to three people to Resources, to call or message them from there. Names and numbers you pick stay on this iPhone. Committed doesn’t read your contact list: you pick each person in the system’s own contact picker, which hands the app that one contact, and Committed keeps only that person’s name and the one number you choose. The app never asks for access to your contacts. These people are never sent to us, and they are not part of Committed’s iCloud sync or backup. Like the rest of the app’s data on your iPhone, they are included when you back up or transfer the iPhone itself with Apple’s own tools. Nothing is called or sent unless you tap Call or Message. You can remove a person in Resources at any time, and “Delete Data on This Device” removes them all.

The calculator on this website

The sobriety date calculator runs in your browser. The date you enter is not saved, transmitted, or added to the page address.

Data retention and deletion

Because your data lives on your device and (optionally) in your own iCloud, you remain in full control of it at all times. You can:

  • Delete individual commitments within the app.
  • Erase your local data using the app’s reset/delete function.
  • Turn off iCloud Sync, and use Delete from iCloud to remove the synced copy (and Delete Old Backup for a backup from an earlier version).
  • Delete the app, which removes its local data from your device.

We do not retain copies of your data. The only thing we hold is feedback you sent us, which we delete if you ask.

Children’s privacy

Committed is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any information from children. Apart from feedback a person chooses to send, the app collects no personal information at all, so no special data is gathered from any user, regardless of age.

Your rights (GDPR, CCPA, and similar laws)

Privacy laws such as the EU GDPR and California’s CCPA give you rights to access, correct, delete, and port your personal data, and to object to its sale. Committed is designed so that these rights are satisfied directly: we hold none of your data apart from feedback you sent us (which we delete on request), you can access and delete all of it yourself within the app, and we never sell your data.

Changes to this policy
