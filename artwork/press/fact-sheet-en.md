# Committed: fact sheet

**Committed** is a private, beautifully made sobriety and commitment tracker for iPhone, Mac and Apple Watch, from an independent developer.

## In one line
Count every day you've kept a commitment and see how far you've come, privately, on iPhone, Mac and Apple Watch.

## Short description (about 50 words)
Committed counts the days since you quit drinking, smoking or anything else, and turns them into something you can see: a field of dots (Matrix), a constellation that climbs with every milestone (Your Sky), or a clean ledger of numbers. It is private by design, with no ads, tracking or accounts.

## Long description
Committed is for anyone keeping a promise to themselves: sobriety from alcohol, quitting nicotine, cutting sugar, stopping gambling. Pick a start date and Committed counts every day since, down to the hour if you like.

Progress is shown three ways. **Matrix** draws one dot for every day, with milestones glowing in a warmer tone. **Your Sky** turns your milestones into a constellation that climbs as you do, with your own milestones as shooting stars. **Ledger** is the quiet option: years, months and days in clean numerals.

Committed lives where you look. Home Screen, Lock Screen and Mac desktop widgets keep your count one glance away, and the Apple Watch app and complications put it on your wrist. Built-in milestones mark the big days (a week, 30 days, 90 days, a year), and you can add your own, like a first sober New Year's or a family wedding.

When a day is hard, Committed helps you find a meeting (AA, NA, SMART Recovery, Al-Anon), a helpline in your country, or someone you trust to call.

It is private by design: no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no account. Your data stays on your devices and, if you turn it on, syncs through your own private iCloud.
