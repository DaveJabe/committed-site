Privacy Policy

Committed · Effective June 30, 2026

Committed does not collect, track, sell, or share any personal data. There are no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking of any kind.

Information stored on your device

The commitments, dates, milestones, and settings you create are stored locally on your device. This information stays on your device unless you choose to enable iCloud backup.

iCloud backup

If you enable backup, your data is synced to your own private iCloud account using Apple’s iCloud (CloudKit). It is stored in your personal iCloud account and governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy. The developer cannot access, view, or retrieve your iCloud data.

Purchases

Committed offers a one-time in-app purchase (Lifetime Unlock) processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. The developer does not receive or store your payment information.

Face ID

If you choose to hide commitments behind Face ID, authentication is performed on-device by Apple’s system. Your biometric data is never accessible to the app and never leaves your device.

What the developer collects

Nothing. The app contains no analytics, no advertising identifiers, and no crash-reporting SDKs, and it makes no network connection to any developer server.

Children’s privacy

Committed is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
